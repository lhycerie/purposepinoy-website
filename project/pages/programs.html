Partnership

Thrift for Good & The HOPE Foundation

Our Thrift for Good initiative combines sustainable fashion with community impact. We organize group thrift trips to source clothing, sell curated pieces online to raise funds, and then donate unsold items to the HOPE Foundation.

Impact: Promoting dignity and confidence for women and girls healing from trauma.

Outreach

Street Children Christmas Toy Drive

Spread joy this December with our road-trip style outreach! The Street Children Christmas Toy Drive is designed to bring festive cheer and inclusion to street children across selected locations.

Impact: Ensuring every child experiences the holiday season with warmth.

Relief Ops

Red Cross PH - Typhoon Uwan Relief

Immediate relief efforts by collaborating with Red Cross Philippines in Mandaluyong. Our focus is on practical assistance through food items and vital hygiene kits for families affected by recent typhoons.

Impact: Providing essential food items and vital hygiene kits.
