Donation Drive

Christmas Donation Drive 2025

We organized a mobilized gift-giving drive to street children in line with the holiday season. Spreading kindness and smiles for those in need.

Makati City, Philippines

About This Event

Amidst 2025 Christmas season, we were able to make a roundtrip from Las Piñas to Makati City, distributing gifts and essentials to street children along the way. Our volunteers helped pack donations, write personalized letters, and personally deliver them — creating moments of joy and connection.

Impact Highlights

  • ✓Over 150 gifts and Christmas cards were hand-prepared and distributed
  • ✓Provided educational books, clothes, toys, and school supplies
  • ✓Mobilized transport to deliver gifts and goods

Community Outreach

Pasong Buaya

Our team visited the Pasong Buaya community to conduct educational workshops and distribute essential supplies. This event brought together volunteers and community members in a meaningful exchange.

Pasong Buaya, Philippines

About This Event

Through interactive sessions and personal connections, we were able to make a lasting impact on the lives of local students and families. Our volunteers worked closely with community leaders to identify needs and deliver meaningful support.

Partnership

ROTARACT X PURPOSE PINOY

In partnership with Rotaract, we launched a community support initiative to provide micro-entrepreneurs and small business owners with resources and mentorship.

Partner: Rotaract Philippines

About This Event

This collaboration brought together young professionals and community members to foster economic empowerment and sustainable livelihoods in underserved areas. We focused on building lasting relationships and providing practical business support.
