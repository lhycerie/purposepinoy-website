Who is
Purpose Pinoy?

Mission

To develop young Filipinos... Into compassionate, capable, and solutions-oriented leaders who can design and implement initiatives that respond to real community needs.

Through accessible volunteer programs, youth-led fundraising, and social entrepreneurship projects, we create avenues for civic engagement.

Our focus: Livelihood (SDG 1) & Education (SDG 8).

Vision

We envision a generation... Of Filipino youth equipped and mobilized to drive sustainable progress.

Through collaborative planning, we build a future where young people play a central role in:

  • No Poverty (SDG 1)
  • Decent Work (SDG 8)

We believe youth leadership is the standard for national development.

Our Story

Sitio Makabuhay, 2023

Sitio Makabuhay — 2023

Sophomore Year

In 2024, after years of online learning, our founder, Athena Hortaleza, joined an outreach visit to Sitio Makabuhay, one of De La Salle Zobel’s partner communities.

There, she spent time with the mothers, women whose days were devoted to caring for their families, often with little opportunity to earn from home.

Detergent Making Workshop

Project: Sheconomy — 2024

Women Empowerment

With raw materials from Cyleina Organics, Purpose Pinoy held its very first event, a detergent-making workshop that provided 77 mothers with livelihood starter kits and guidance on how to begin reselling from home.

The project raised ₱20,450 in funding, turning an everyday household item into a means for income and empowerment.

Current Mission

Pasong Buaya 2025: Waste 2 Wash

Today, Purpose Pinoy’s program partners with 8 local eateries (carinderias) in Pasong Buaya to collect used cooking oil and turn it into Multi-Purpose Cleaning Bars.

With 15 enthusiastic participants, this act of sustainability reduces waste while creating detergent bars that do more than clean, they create livelihoods and promote a circular and sustainable economy.

"Every bar sold is a step toward empowerment!"
