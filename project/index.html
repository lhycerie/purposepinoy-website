Our Mission

Igniting Entrepreneurship To Break the Cycle.

Welcome to Purpose Pinoy. We are a dynamic youth movement with a singular mission: to empower the next generation of Filipinos to be catalysts of economic change.

We believe that poverty is a challenge best fought with innovation and enterprise. We focus on igniting entrepreneurship to directly break the cycle of poverty in our communities.

About Us
Purpose Pinoy is a passionate, student-driven volunteer organization dedicated to helping people in need. We focus on community service, organizing outreaches, and running donation drives, with a special focus on helping street children and orphanages.

Involvement & Departments

When you apply, you can join various teams that fit your skills, including:

  • Operations: Planning logistics and executing outreaches on the ground.
  • External Affairs: Connecting with sponsors and handling public relations.
  • Creatives: Designing visual content and social media branding.
  • IT: Technical support and digital platform management.
  • Internal Affairs: Organising team-building and keeping the org running smoothly.
We utilize our Meetings channels for online planning and committee sync-ups. Actual outreaches, gift-giving, and specific activities are scheduled as face-to-face events depending on the project.
